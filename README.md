# My Favorite Filipino Movie
## Four Sisters Before the Wedding
**Genre:** Family Drama, Comedy
**Director:** Mae Cruz-Alviar
**Writer:** Vanessa R. Valdez
**Release Date:** December 11, 2020
**Running Time:** 116 minutes
**Country:** Philippines
**Language:** Filipino
**Production Company:** Star Cinema
---
## 1. About the Movie
*Four Sisters Before the Wedding* is a Filipino family drama-comedy film that serves as a prequel to the movie *Four Sisters and a Wedding*. It tells the story of the younger Salazar siblings and shows what their lives were like before the events of the first movie.
The story takes place in 2003 and follows Teddie, Bobbie, Alex, and Gabbie as they experience different challenges while growing up. Although they have different personalities, dreams, and opinions, they share a strong bond as sisters.
Their lives become more complicated when they discover that their parents, Grace and Caloy, are experiencing problems in their marriage. Worried about their family, the four sisters decide to work together to find a way to save their parents' relationship.
The movie highlights important values such as love, understanding, communication, forgiveness, and the importance of family.

## 2. Main Cast and Characters
The movie features several talented Filipino actors who portray the younger versions of the Salazar family.
**Charlie Dizon** as Teddie Salazar
**Alexa Ilacad** as Bobbie Salazar
**Gillian Vicencio** as Alex Salazar
**Belle Mariano** as Gabbie Salazar
**Clarence Delgado** as CJ Salazar
**Carmina Villaroel** as Grace Salazar
**Dominic Ochoa** as Caloy Salazar
Each character has a different personality and perspective, which makes the story more interesting and relatable.

## 3. Summary of the Story
The movie follows the four Salazar sisters as they deal with their personal struggles and family responsibilities. Teddie, Bobbie, Alex, and Gabbie have different dreams and ways of handling situations. Because of their differences, they sometimes experience misunderstandings and arguments.
Things become more difficult when they discover that their parents are having serious problems in their marriage. Worried that their family might fall apart, the sisters decide to work together to bring their parents closer and save their relationship.
As they try to solve their family's problems, they also begin to understand their own feelings and the importance of supporting one another. Their experiences show that every family member has personal struggles, and sometimes, understanding and listening are more important than simply trying to fix everything.

## 4. Why I Like This Movie
I like *Four Sisters Before the Wedding* because it presents a realistic picture of family relationships. It shows that every family experiences problems, misunderstandings, and disagreements, even when everything seems fine on the outside.
I also enjoy how the movie combines funny moments with emotional scenes. Some parts make me laugh, while others remind me of how important it is to appreciate and understand the people around me.
Another reason I like this movie is that the characters have different personalities and ways of expressing their feelings. Their experiences show that people may not always agree with each other, but they can still learn to understand and support one another.
Most importantly, the movie reminds me that family is not about being perfect. It is about accepting each other's differences, learning from mistakes, and choosing to stay connected despite challenges.

## 5. My Favorite Character
### Bobbie Salazar
**Bobbie Salazar** is my favorite character because she is thoughtful, responsible, and someone who tries to understand the people around her.
I find her character interesting because she also experiences personal struggles while dealing with the problems within her family. Despite the challenges she faces, she continues to show care and concern for the people she loves.
I appreciate her character because she reminds me that everyone has their own struggles, even those who seem strong and responsible. Sometimes, the people who are always trying to understand others also need someone who will listen to and understand them.

## 7. Lessons I Learned from the Movie
There are several lessons that I learned from watching this movie:
**Family is not always perfect.** Every family experiences problems, but these challenges do not mean that love is no longer present.
**Communication is important.** We should learn to express our feelings instead of keeping everything to ourselves.
**Understanding matters.** Before judging someone, we should try to understand their situation and the reasons behind their actions.
**Everyone has personal struggles.** Even the people closest to us may be dealing with problems that we do not know about.
**Forgiveness helps relationships grow.** Learning to forgive allows people to move forward and rebuild their relationships.
**Appreciate your family.** We should not wait for difficult situations before showing our loved ones how much they mean to us.
